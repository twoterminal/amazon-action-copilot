Privacy Policy

Amazon Action Copilot · Last updated: October 4, 2026

Amazon Action Copilot is a seller decision-support application designed to help Amazon sellers identify important business issues and prioritize operational actions.

1. Information We Collect

When an Amazon seller authorizes the application, we may receive permitted Amazon seller information through the Amazon Selling Partner API (SP-API), including sales, orders, inventory, product performance, and other business information required for authorized functionality.

We do not intentionally request or process Amazon buyer personally identifiable information (PII) when it is not required for the application's functionality.

2. How We Use Information

Amazon information is used to analyze seller business performance, identify operational signals, calculate priorities, and provide recommendations. We do not sell Amazon information or use it for purposes unrelated to the authorized functionality.

3. Amazon Data

Amazon information obtained through SP-API is used only to provide authorized seller-facing functionality and handled in accordance with applicable Amazon requirements and policies.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or provide Amazon information to unauthorized third parties. Service providers, if required to operate the application, will receive only information necessary for authorized functionality and will be subject to appropriate safeguards.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect application credentials and seller information. Access is restricted according to business need and authorized functionality.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Amazon information is retained only as long as necessary to provide authorized functionality and meet applicable requirements. Information no longer required will be securely deleted or otherwise disposed of.

7. Seller Control

The application provides advisory recommendations. Sellers remain responsible for reviewing recommendations and taking actions within their Amazon accounts.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when necessary. The current version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy or data-handling questions, please use the contact information provided on the application's official website.
