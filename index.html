Instead of requiring sellers to review multiple reports and dashboards, Action Copilot is designed to surface the most important operational signals.

02

Advisory, not automatic

Amazon Action Copilot is designed as a decision-support application. Recommendations require seller review. The application does not independently change product listings, prices, advertising settings, inventory settings, orders, or account configuration.

Currently in development

Amazon Action Copilot is currently being developed and tested. Amazon-connected functionality will be provided only after the applicable authorization and integration requirements are completed.
