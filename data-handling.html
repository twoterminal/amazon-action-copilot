Amazon Data Handling Policy

Amazon Action Copilot · Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy describes how Amazon information is collected, processed, stored, used, shared, retained, and disposed of by Amazon Action Copilot.

1. Collection

Amazon information is collected directly from Amazon through the Selling Partner API after the relevant selling partner authorizes the application. We do not retrieve Amazon information from unrelated external or non-Amazon sources.

2. Processing

Information is processed to normalize seller business data, calculate metrics, identify significant changes or risks, prioritize issues, and generate seller-facing recommendations.

3. Intended Data

The application is designed to use business information such as sales, orders, inventory, and permitted seller performance information. The core functionality does not require Amazon buyer PII.

4. Storage

Amazon information may be stored only where necessary for authorized functionality, application state, analysis, or applicable requirements. Production storage will use appropriate access controls and security measures.

5. Use

Amazon information is used solely to provide functionality authorized by the seller, including the “What Should I Do Today?” decision-support experience.

6. Sharing

Amazon information is not sold or rented and is not intentionally disclosed to unauthorized third parties. Any necessary service-provider access will be limited to the minimum required and protected appropriately.

7. Security

Application credentials and sensitive configuration values are kept server-side and are not intended to be exposed in client-side code or public repositories. Access is limited according to authorized responsibilities and business need.

8. Retention and Disposal

Amazon information is retained only as reasonably necessary for authorized functionality and applicable requirements, then securely deleted or disposed of when no longer required.

9. No Automated Amazon Account Changes

Amazon Action Copilot is an advisory decision-support application. Recommendations do not by themselves modify seller listings, prices, advertising settings, inventory settings, orders, or account configuration.
